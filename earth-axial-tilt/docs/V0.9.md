# v0.9 — Compare Lab and coupled motion

## Implemented

A and B differ in obliquity only. The date, rotation phase, geographic point, model, heat storage, camera and layer share one source. Presets set A=23.44° and B=0°/45°/90°. Both angles can also be entered directly. Synchronization cannot be disabled in this version.

One WebGLRenderer and scene render two scissor viewports, applying B only for its pass and restoring A afterward. Geometries and imagery are not duplicated into a second context. Desktop uses side-by-side views; narrow screens stack. Both close-up/orbit and Focus work. These are separate hypothetical worlds; the two Sun images do not represent two stars in one shared physical simulation.

The location panel describes A. Its annual dashed curve changes to B during comparison, using the same axis scale. The daily graph remains A only. Atlas remains A versus 23.44° and displays that distinction explicitly. The comparison table is A, B and **A−B** for daylight, daily solar and daily-mean-style temperature.

B has its own bounded thermal request lifecycle. A=B reuses A's solution. A missing or stale temperature cannot silently become a current value. The solar/daylight comparisons remain usable when B's worker fails; a retry is available. Compare closes back to the original single-world settings.

## Coupled clock and coordinate correction

The pre-v0.9 isolated animations used positive Y spin but negative Y orbital angular momentum at zero tilt. That convention was acceptable only as two separately controlled diagrams, not a faithful coupled prograde clock. This release corrects the frame:

- Sun direction `[cosλ,0,-sinλ]`.
- Surface transform `Rx(-obliquity) * Ry(rotation)`.
- Planet centre in orbit view is `-14 * Sun direction`.
- Positive eastward spin and orbit both have +Y angular momentum.

Daily and seasonal scalar astronomy, the solar constant and the climate equations are unchanged. The arbitrary initial spin phase's subsolar longitude is not promised identical to the older mirrored diagram. The default camera is mirrored to retain a useful daytime initial view.

A model year is exactly 365 **mean solar days**, with 366 prograde inertial turns. `drotation/dday = 360*366/365` degrees. At speed1 the model advances 0.1 day per real second; speed4/12 scales both rates. An apparent solar clock is not uniform for nonzero obliquity. Existing undefined polar meridians remain undefined.

Play day still holds the date and takes 30 real seconds per turn at speed1. Play year still holds rotation. Coupled is a third mode, not a change to either independent experiment. Visibility transitions do not accumulate hidden-tab time. See [SCIENCE](SCIENCE.md).
