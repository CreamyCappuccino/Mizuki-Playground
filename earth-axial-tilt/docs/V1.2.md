# v1.2 — Earth Orbit Mechanics

Feature track: **1.2.0-rc.1**. The physical iPhone Safari gate and separate deployment approval remain open. This release contains no other-planet climate, land/ocean mapping or nonlinear feedback model.

## Implemented

Eccentricity 0–0.3, Kepler time evolution and apsis/season markers, distance and normalized speed diagnostics, inverse-square TOA forcing, apparent Sun perihelion longitude and independent static axis azimuth. Individual A/B orbital conditions feed the globe, instantaneous/day/year curves, comparison and thermal workers. The original circular shortcut preserves existing scalar and thermal results.

The optional left-column Orbit laboratory includes Earth-like/circular/summer/winter presets, side selection, A→B orbit copying, apsis shortcuts, invalid-input recovery and bilingual explanation. Three new question presets join the seven original circular experiments. Large controls wrap rather than shrink. The context guide explains the day-80 anchor, circle/zero-tilt degeneracies and the distinction between static axis direction and physical precession history.

Portable state is schema 2. Both A/B orbits round-trip; schema 1 migrates to the classic circle. Unknown future versions and invalid owned fields are rejected atomically. Every annual thermal forcing is recomputed when orbit conditions change; previous queued replies cannot replace the newest configuration. Atlas remains A against 23.44° on A's orbit, not B.

## Verification record

Independent numerical audit of the inherited physics revision `d8c3d28`: 12,753 scalar assertions, 756 exact legacy scalar comparisons, three complete 365×90 thermal arrays byte-identical to the v1.1 circle, and twelve converged extreme-orbit thermal cases. These are numerical checks, not thousands of separately named unit tests or evidence of a physical iPhone test.

The runnable regression suite currently has **130 unit tests** (including 16 independent-orbit/state/provenance cases), plus eight new v1.2 real-browser scenarios. Documentation checks, TypeScript and production build are required, followed by the full Chromium suite and selected macOS WebKit suites at the identical Git commit. Only actual completed run results count as browser verification; see current branch/main CI for status. Original golden images are not automatically replaced.

Browser scenarios exercise apsis diagnostics, A=B after copying an orbit, rapid-edit stale-result rejection, old/new saved state, Japanese Large phone layouts and context guide, Focus/display invariance, zero tilt and extreme warnings, and the Atlas reference. Screenshots are retained for review. Administration policy can prevent local browser access; CI browsers and their artifacts provide the executable verification in that environment.

## Deliberate limits and next step

The model period is fixed at 365 days and the semimajor axis at 1 au. All worlds anchor their own spring reference to day 80; comparisons are not simultaneous historical epochs. Axis azimuth is static. High-eccentricity thermal values are labelled extrapolations, not predictions. No physical iPhone Safari acceptance or public deployment is claimed.

After this version's same-HEAD verification closes, follow [ROADMAP](../ROADMAP.md) to Earth Climate Geography v1.3. Do not declare the next version complete from a feature plan alone.
