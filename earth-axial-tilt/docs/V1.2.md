# v1.2 — Orbit Mechanics Lab

## Scope

v1.2 extends Earth Classic without replacing it. A world now has obliquity plus an orbit: eccentricity, inertial perihelion longitude and inertial axis orientation. A 365-day model clock advances uniformly in mean anomaly. Kepler's equation gives true longitude and distance; TOA solar forcing is multiplied by 1/r²; relative speed follows vis-viva.

The explicit educational eccentricity range is 0–0.6. This is a concept lab, not a calendar ephemeris. Model day 80 remains mean longitude 0. At e=0, distance, flux and speed are exactly 1, the old circular results are retained, and perihelion direction is physically degenerate.

## Shared coordinate frame

The prograde orbital frame uses +Y angular momentum and Earth-to-Sun direction `[cos(lambda), 0, -sin(lambda)]`. Perihelion longitude, true longitude and axis orientation are measured in this one frame. Earth presentation applies `Ry(axis) * Rx(-tilt) * Ry(spin)`. Tilt magnitude and axis orientation are separate controls.

## Surfaces

Orbit overview draws the eccentric path, dynamic quarter-season markers, and perihelion/aphelion when defined. The readouts expose relative distance, flux and speed. Main metrics, instantaneous solar, daily and annual graphs, Season Atlas, comparison and Thermal EBM all use the same orbit forcing.

Compare A/B permits independent tilt and orbit parameters while retaining the shared model phase, rotation, location, climate model and heat storage. Presets cover circular/high-e, opposite axis orientation and opposite perihelion seasons. A mismatched thermal result remains unavailable instead of being shown under a new orbit.

## Persistence and compatibility

Experiment schema 2 stores both worlds' orbit parameters. Schema 1 migrates to Earth Classic. Invalid values, duplicate owned URL keys, version conflicts and unknown versions reject atomically. Display preferences and calculated thermal arrays are not imported.

## Boundaries

No geography-dependent heat storage, nonlinear climate feedback, live ephemeris or habitability claim is added. Sizes remain illustrative. Real-device iPhone/Safari acceptance and public deployment remain separate, uncompleted gates.
