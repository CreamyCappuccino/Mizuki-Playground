## Primary-source provenance (checked 2026-10-01)

- [NASA Mars Fact Sheet](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/marsfact.html):
  model period 686.98 Earth days, obliquity 25.19 degrees, sidereal rotation
  24.6229 Earth hours and mean solar day 24.6597 Earth hours. The J2000 element
  eccentricity 0.09341233 is rounded to 0.0934 in this educational model.
- The same NASA sheet gives J2000 semimajor axis 1.52366231 au. The already-used
  model scale 1.523679 au is retained as a fixed teaching approximation (relative
  difference about 0.0011%), **not** attributed to NASA as an exact tabulated value.
- [NASA GISS Mars24 technical notes](https://www.giss.nasa.gov/tools/mars24/help/notes.html):
  northern spring/summer/autumn/winter are Ls 0/90/180/270 degrees. Perihelion Ls is
  251 degrees at the reference year 2000; the lab freezes that reference and does
  not implement its secular rate. The geometric declination formula and distinction
  between Earth hours and a 24-part Mars clock are also defined there.
- [JPL Kepler formulation](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/planets/approx_pos.html):
  Kepler equation and orbital-plane coordinates support the two-body geometry.
  This lab does not implement the page's dated ephemeris or claim its error bounds.

Earth retains its historical 365-day model year and 24-hour mean solar day. Mars
uses a fixed 686.98-day period, not an annually changing tropical ephemeris. The
separately listed sidereal period is descriptive; no new coupled-spin animation
is claimed. All displayed h are Earth hours; Mars daylight is not a count on a
24-Mars-hour clock. TOA flux here is ray-normal, not local horizontal daily mean.
The horizon-all-day degeneracy reports half the world's solar day by convention,
not a claim of effective illumination for those hours.

## Scope of the alpha

The common definitions and Earth adapter are an additive first stage. Existing
Earth 3D/climate/Atlas workspaces and formats stay on their accepted implementation;
this is not a completed rewrite of all Earth internals. The new page compares
astronomy readouts. It does not add a Mars 3D globe, Mars temperature solver, or
reinterpret old Earth URLs as lossy Planet URLs. These boundaries remain visible
rather than treating all future roadmap items as completed.

## Verification evidence

The hardening commit must pass full same-HEAD CI before main advances. Cloud TypeScript strict checking succeeds, but this runtime blocks Chromium
localhost navigation (ERR_BLOCKED_BY_ADMINISTRATOR). No workaround is used and
no cloud-browser success is claimed; Vite production build and both browser

separate acceptance items. Numerical consistency is not climate validation.

A separate Python/SciPy quadrature checked elapsed time for Earth and Mars at
180 seasonal angles each (360 samples). It integrates
`P/(2*pi)*(1-e*e)**1.5/(1+e*cos(Ls-Ls_peri))**2` from spring to the selected Ls,
rather than using the production eccentric-anomaly conversion. Maximum observed
difference was 7.958078640513122e-13 Earth days (criterion 1e-8). This is a numerical
consistency check against the fixed model, not an ephemeris accuracy estimate.
