Temperature estimate

Mean temperature estimate, not the daytime high. Illustrative latitude-band model; not fitted to local weather.

What do these numbers mean?

Temperature represents a typical daily-mean-style estimate including both day and night. It is not a maximum, minimum, or feels-like temperature. Places at the same latitude share the same estimate: oceans, altitude, clouds, and city effects are not modelled.

The annual summary averages all 365 model days. The comparison line uses the same simplified model at 23.44°, not observed climate normals. This version retains v0.1's illustrative temperature coefficients; it is not calibrated to Taipei or Singapore.

Solar is daily-mean incoming energy at the top of the atmosphere (TOA), not ground-level sunlight. Daylight uses a point Sun without refraction. At an exact all-day horizon, 12 h is a reporting convention and incoming energy is zero.

The globe is Earth-centred: the moving Sun indicates direction, not a scale orbit. The circular 365-day model does not reproduce exact calendar dates of the equinoxes. No time-of-day rotation is simulated, so subsolar longitude is illustrative.
